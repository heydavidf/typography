The places we make room for

Words by Åsa Öberg · Fictional example

When the last shop on the corner closed, its windows stayed lit. Neighbours began leaving chairs outside, then books, then an invitation to sit. A shared place was taking shape through a series of small decisions.

You could arrive without a plan, read for an hour, or talk to someone you would otherwise pass on the pavement. Its usefulness came from the time people were willing to give it.

Learning to leave a little space

On Wednesday evenings, the group meets in Örebro. They discuss opening hours and who should water the plants. For now, the door is open, the kettle works, and somebody remembers your name.
