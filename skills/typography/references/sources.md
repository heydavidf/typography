# Sources and interpretation

Public links checked 6 October 2026. This skill combines targeted reading, web standards and design judgment. It is not a systematic readability study or a claim of complete book coverage. The books below are influences, not prerequisites; no PDFs, reproduced pages or font assets are bundled.

## Books and teaching

- **Josef Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems in Graphic Design / Raster Systeme für die visuelle Gestaltung*, Niggli.** [Publisher](https://niggli.ch/products/rastersysteme-fur-die-visuelle-gestaltung). Informs the relationship between content, format, measure, leading, margins and the type area. Responsive spans and fluid margins are this skill's interpretation, not the book's browser advice.
- **Elliot Jay Stocks, *Advanced Typography for the Web & Print* (2017 workshop/talk).** [Author's introduction](https://elliotjaystocks.com/blog/advanced-typography-for-the-web-and-print/). Informs role-based pairing, spacing and composing outwards from type. Pairing ideas encountered through this teaching also credit Tim Brown, Erik Spiekermann and Jason Santa Maria; this is not a claim of direct study of all their original work.
- **Erik Spiekermann, *Stop Stealing Sheep & find out how type works*, fourth edition.** [Official Google-hosted edition](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/fonts.google.com/en//knowledge/stop_stealing_sheep.pdf). Informs purpose, selection, rhythm, spacing and variable-font thinking. Historical print advice is contextual and does not establish web accessibility rules.
- **Robert Bringhurst, *The Elements of Typographic Style*, Hartley & Marks (second edition consulted).** [Publisher](https://www.hartleyandmarks.com/Hartley-%26-Marks-Publishers). Informs text texture, measure, page/textblock distinctions and margins. Historic ratios are studies to try, not guarantees of harmony or web requirements.
- **Ellen Lupton, *Thinking with Type*, Princeton Architectural Press (second revised edition consulted).** [Current publisher page](https://www.chroniclebooks.com/products/thinking-with-type-1), which presents the third edition. Informs spacing, leading, alignment and hierarchy. The linked edition and the edition consulted differ.
- **Richard Hunt, *Advanced Typography: From Knowledge to Mastery* (2020), Bloomsbury Visual Arts.** [Publisher](https://www.bloomsbury.com/uk/advanced-typography-9781350055919/). Informs final-environment testing, text/display craft and optical spacing. This book is not current browser-support documentation.
- **Kimberly Elam, *Grid Systems: Principles of Organizing Type*, Princeton Architectural Press.** [Bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Grid_Systems.html?id=ROY7BTddbjgC). Informs grouping, negative space, axes, edges and variation within a grid. A static composition does not demonstrate responsive text flow.
- **Timothy Samara, *Making and Breaking the Grid*, third edition, Rockport/Quarto.** [Publisher](https://www.quarto.com/books/9780760381939/making-and-breaking-the-grid-third-edition). Informs grid anatomy, active space and deliberate breaks. Selected web examples do not establish browser implementation behavior.

## Supplementary vocabulary

[Pixso, Building Better UI Grid Design](https://pixso.net/tips/grid-design/) was a supplementary vocabulary influence. Treat its introductory grid names cautiously: flowlines and text baselines differ, equal gutters are optional, and historic ratios are not mandatory rules. It is not the implementation or accessibility authority.

## Current implementation guidance

- [Google Design: How to Choose a Web Font](https://design.google/library/choosing-web-fonts-beginners-guide): selection by purpose, scope, functionality and contextual pairing.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and its explanations of [contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [resize text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html), [reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) and [text spacing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/text-spacing.html): targeted measurable checks with specific exceptions.
- [CSS Fonts Level 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-fonts-4/), a working draft, and [MDN variable fonts](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Fonts/Variable_fonts): descriptors, supported axes and optical sizing. Verify actual assets and target browsers.
- [CSS Grid Layout Level 2](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-grid-2/): layout mechanics, separate from aesthetic judgment.

## Interpretation and rights

Comfortable measure, a restrained family count and a scale are practical starting choices, not natural laws. Pairing explanations are reasoned proposals, not scientifically validated scores. Typography and accessibility need separate evidence. Responsive recommendations and the synthetic examples are original applications of these ideas.
